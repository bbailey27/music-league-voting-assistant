# Uncertainty

`?` on the music score means the **score** could move up or down — not a bias either way.

When `?` comes **after another modifier**, it qualifies **that modifier**, not the score:

- `75+?` — unsure about the `+`, not the 75
- `7-?` — unsure about the `-`, not the 70
- `74 play?` — unsure about playlist add, not the 74

Do not assume midpoint, upward bias, or downward bias for score uncertainty.

When a song with score-`?` sits comfortably inside a tier, leave it there.

When a song with score-`?` sits near a tier boundary, compare it against adjacent
candidates and ask for clarification if needed.

Examples:

- `73?` among other 73 songs: leave grouped.
- `73?` near a cutoff between 72.5 and 73.5: may require review.
