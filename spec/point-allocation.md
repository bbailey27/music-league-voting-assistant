# Point Allocation

Adaptive rather than formulaic.

Consider:
- total points
- number of songs
- quality spread
- score density
- fit weighting
- points-to-songs ratio

Example:
10 points across 20 songs implies a 0.5 average and often leads to many 0s and 1s.

Distributions may be:
- compressed
- balanced
- top-heavy

Avoid artificial equality.
